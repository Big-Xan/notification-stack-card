# Roadmap

## Next

- **Keyboard access**: open the stack, sub-stacks and full messages with Tab and Enter/Space, with focus outlines and `aria-expanded`.
- **Editor warnings** for duplicate IDs, unknown groups, missing entities and invalid times.
- **Automated checks**: a GitHub workflow for HACS validation and the card's browser tests.

## Ideas

- **Group-level clear**: an X on an open sub-stack's heading to dismiss the whole group.
- **Template editing in place**: edit a template's groups and alerts from any card that uses it.
- **Quiet hours from a schedule helper**, as an alternative to fixed times.
